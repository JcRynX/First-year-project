MY TOP 3 KPOP IDOLS

BLACKPINK

Blackpink (Korean: 블랙핑크, stylized in all caps or as BLΛƆKPIИK) is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment. 
            The group is composed of four members: Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. Regarded by various publications as the "biggest girl group in the world", they are recognized 
            as a leading force in the Korean Wave and an ambassador of the "girl crush" concept in K-pop, which explores themes of self-confidence and female empowerment.

ITZY

ITZY is a 5-member South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment. 
            They debuted on February 12, 2019, with their single album "It'z Different" 
            and its lead single "Dalla Dalla". The group is known for their "teen crush" 
            concept, exploring themes of independence and self-love in their music. ITZY 
            has achieved significant success, including winning multiple Rookie of the Year 
            awards and selling over one million copies of their fifth EP, "Checkmate". 
            Their fandom name, MIDZY, symbolizes the trust and bond between the group and their fans.

TWICE

Twice (Korean: 트와이스; RR: Teuwaiseu; commonly stylized in all caps) is a South Korean girl 
            group formed by JYP Entertainment. The group is composed of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, 
            Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. Twice was formed under the television 
            program Sixteen (2015) and debuted on October 20, 2015, with the extended play (EP) The Story 
            Begins, and has received the honorific title of "The Nation's Girl Group" in their home country.
