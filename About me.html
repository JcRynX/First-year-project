ABOUT ME

Jc Ryan Arcenas is a 20 years old BSCS student. He is the third child of Josefino Arcenas and Rosa Arcenas. He has a brother named Jay-ar Arcenas and a sister Jullan Rose Arcenas.

He is a resident of Cosme 1 Santol Street, Admiral Village, Talon Tres, Las Piñas City. He finished his primary and elementary education at Talon Tres Elementary School. He graduated high school at Las Piñas City Technical-Vocational High School.

For Senior High School, he continued his studies at Saint Francis of Assisi College – Las Piñas Campus and took the ICT strand. Though he had second doubts, he pursued ICT due to his strong interest in computers.

He is a member of the Boy Scouts of the Philippines. He is now a first-year college student taking Bachelor of Science in Computer Studies. The road ahead is long and not easy, but he is determined to keep striving to achieve his dreams.
